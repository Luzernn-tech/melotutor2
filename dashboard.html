How sessions work

  1. Every session is 60 minutesOne session is one full hour with the learner.
  2. Pick how it startsAuto starts at the scheduled time. Manual lets you tap Start session from 15 minutes before.
  3. Choose transcriptionTurn it on and the lesson transcript is saved to the session once it ends.
  4. MeloTutor verifies itAfter verification the earning moves to your available balance.

How earnings work

  1. Teach a sessionSessions follow the learner’s brief.
  2. MeloTutor verifies itEarnings are recorded only after a session is verified.
  3. Money clearsVerified earnings move from pending to available.
  4. You withdrawPick a saved payout account, enter its password and request your money.
Payments are made only through the official MeloTutor process. Never send money to an unverified number.
