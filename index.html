TUTOR EXPECTATIONS

Professional Tutoring Standards.

Every tutor is expected to meet these basic standards when accepting and completing a MeloTutor task.

REQUIREMENT 01

Prepare and Teach with Confidence

Have a strong command of Swahili and prepare for each lesson in advance. Review the learner's stated goals, plan how you will use the session and be ready to explain concepts clearly.

REQUIREMENT 02

Complete the Full Session

Each accepted tutoring task has a minimum duration of 60 minutes. Stay engaged for the agreed session and complete the required lesson. Tasks left unfinished may be marked incomplete and will not qualify for payment.

REQUIREMENT 03

Be Punctual and Accountable

Join on time and give the learner your full attention. Avoid unnecessary delays, repeated lateness or leaving a session early. If an unavoidable issue arises, communicate it promptly through the appropriate platform channel.

REQUIREMENT 04

Use a Stable Internet Connection

When logged in and especially during a tutoring session, make sure you are in an area with a stable network connection. Choose a suitable environment so interruptions do not affect the lesson.

Completion and payment: Payment applies to eligible tutoring tasks completed in line with the task instructions and platform terms. An unfinished or unverified task may be recorded as incomplete and is not payable.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? Answered.

Essential information about teaching Swahili through MeloTutor.

What is MeloTutor?

MeloTutor is a platform focused on connecting people who can teach Swahili with people looking for Swahili language instruction.

What do I need to get started?

You need a strong command of Swahili, a phone or computer, a stable internet connection and the ability to prepare and deliver structured lessons. Each accepted tutoring task has a minimum duration of 60 minutes. Tutors are expected to be punctual, complete the full session and follow the task instructions. Unfinished or unverified tasks may be marked incomplete and will not qualify for payment. Review the full tutor standards before accepting a task.

What are the community guidelines?

Treat learners and other tutors with respect. Keep communication professional and relevant to the lesson, respect privacy, and do not share personal or session information without permission. Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten or mislead anyone. Attend accepted sessions on time, follow the agreed lesson scope and use the platform channels for tutoring-related communication. Reports of misconduct or repeated failure to meet task commitments may be reviewed under the platform rules.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The one-time KSh 500 payment is required to register and activate your tutor account and access MeloTutor. It does not buy learner bookings or guarantee income. Earnings come from eligible tutoring services you provide. Review the applicable terms before paying.

How do tutors receive payment?

Tutors receive payment for eligible tutoring services completed under the platform terms. Rates, payment conditions and available withdrawal methods are shown through the platform.

How can I withdraw my earnings?

Eligible earnings can be withdrawn using the payment methods supported by the platform. Available options and withdrawal requirements may vary by country.

Does registration guarantee tutoring work?

No. Registration gives you access to the platform process, but it does not guarantee learner requests, bookings or income. Opportunities depend on learner demand, your suitability and the arrangements agreed for each lesson.
