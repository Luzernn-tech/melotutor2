What is MeloTutor?

MeloTutor is a platform focused on connecting people who can teach Swahili with people looking for Swahili language instruction.

Who can become a Swahili tutor?

People with a good command of Swahili and the ability to explain the language to learners can explore tutoring through MeloTutor.

How do I get started?

Create an account using the registration link and follow the applicable onboarding process.

Earnings are associated with eligible tutoring activity. Applicable rates, requirements and payment conditions are provided through the platform.

How can I withdraw my earnings?

Eligible earnings can be withdrawn using the payment methods supported by the platform. Available options and withdrawal requirements may vary by country.

Can I teach Swahili online?

MeloTutor focuses on Swahili tutoring. Follow the platform's available tutoring process to understand how lessons are offered.
