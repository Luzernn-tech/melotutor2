    Frequently asked questions

    Why do I enter my name, email and date when requesting a slot?
    Your details are saved with your reservation reference (#MT-XXXXXXXX) so your session schedule and learner match can be confirmed in your dashboard.
    What does the tutor certificate fee cover?
    The nominal $1 USD fee covers secure identity verification, platform credential generation, and instant anti-spam routing so only verified, dedicated instructors connect with global students.
    How and when do tutors get paid?
    Earnings are credited upon completing each 60-minute session and can be withdrawn directly via M-Pesa and mobile money.

    Platform governance

    Terms & conditions

    1. Independent tutor status & eligibility

    Tutors registered through MeloTutor operate as independent language instructors. Applicants must have strong spoken and written fluency in Swahili, be at least 18 years of age, and submit accurate identity details during registration.

    2. Session completion & payout verification

    Tutor earnings are tied to completed sessions of at least 60 minutes delivered in line with the learner’s brief. Withdrawals are processed directly to your verified mobile money account.

    3. Certification & standards

    Certified tutors maintain high teaching standards, punctuality, and clear audio/video quality across all scheduled 1-on-1 Swahili sessions.
