    Tutor Help Center

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I enter my name, email address, and date when reserving?
    Entering your details creates a provisional hold reference (#MT-XXXX) and links your saved shortlist to your profile. Once you complete your tutor application on Mulaearn, your reserved learner requests carry over so you can proceed immediately.
    Do I need to complete registration on Mulaearn to claim a slot?
    Yes. Reserving a learner on MeloTutor places a temporary hold on the slot. You must complete your tutor registration and sign in on Mulaearn to finalize the booking, conduct the session, and receive payouts.
    What does the one-time KSh 500 registration fee cover?
    The one-time KSh 500 fee covers tutor identity verification and onboarding access to the platform. It does not purchase guaranteed bookings or fixed income; tutors earn through eligible, completed 60-minute Swahili lessons.
    How and when do tutors get paid?
    Once a scheduled 60-minute lesson is completed and verified, the session payout is credited to your balance and can be withdrawn via M-Pesa or supported bank rails.

    Platform Governance

    Terms & Conditions

    1. Independent Tutor Status & Eligibility

    Tutors registered through MeloTutor and Mulaearn operate as independent language instructors. Applicants must possess strong spoken and written fluency in Swahili, be at least 18 years of age, and submit accurate identity details during registration.

    2. Session Completion & Payout Verification

    Tutor earnings are strictly tied to eligible, completed tutoring sessions of at least 60 minutes delivered in accordance with the learner’s brief. Missed sessions, unverified lessons, or sessions cut short without learner consent are marked incomplete and do not qualify for payout.

    3. Registration Fee & Non-Guarantee Policy

    The one-time KSh 500 onboarding fee covers profile verification and access to the MeloTutor network and Mulaearn tutor workspace. Platform access does not guarantee a minimum volume of student bookings or fixed income; demand depends on learner schedules and tutor availability.

    4. Code of Conduct, Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention

    Tutors must maintain professional conduct, respect learner privacy, and keep all student contact details confidential. Soliciting learners outside official platform channels or bypassing booking procedures is strictly prohibited and may result in permanent account suspension.
